Frequently asked

Is my data private?

Yes — completely. Plate is local-first: your plates, recipes, photos, comments and messages live only on your device. We run no server and collect no data. See our Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account?

No. You can continue as a guest, or use Sign in with Apple purely to personalise your local profile name — nothing is transmitted anywhere.

What is Plate Pro?

An optional upgrade that unlocks the analytics suite — views, follower growth, top plates, reach and recipe performance. It comes as a monthly or yearly subscription (with a 7-day free trial), or a one-time lifetime purchase. Cooking, posting and recipes are free forever.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your device open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Plate, and manage or cancel there. You can also tap Restore Purchases on the Plate Pro screen to recover a purchase on a new device.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the ••• menu on any dish to report it or block its author, or long-press a comment to report it. Manage blocked accounts under Settings → Blocked accounts.

How do I get my data out?

Open Settings → Data Export to generate a readable JSON file of everything you've made, handed straight to the share sheet.
