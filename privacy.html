Dishery

Privacy Policy · Effective July 16, 2026

Dishery collects no data. Everything you create stays on your device.

Dishery ("the app") is developed by Hugo Moriceau ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Dishery.

1. The local-first principle

Your profile, dishes, photos, recipes, comments, boards, messages, activity history, streaks and settings are stored in a database on your device (Apple's SwiftData framework) and nowhere else. We operate no server, and the app sends none of your content to us or anyone. The "community" you see — chefs, recipes, comments — is demonstration content bundled with the app so it feels alive from first launch; interacting with it happens entirely on your device.

2. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, transmit, sell, rent or share any personal data. Dishery contains:

  • No analytics or telemetry — no usage statistics, crash reporters or measurement SDKs.
  • No advertising — no ad networks, no advertising identifiers (IDFA), no tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs that receive data. Built exclusively on Apple system frameworks.
  • No developer server — there is no account database because there are no accounts on our side.

3. On-device processing

Some features process sensitive inputs — always locally, never leaving your device:

  • Camera & photos. Stored in the app's local database. Food-tag suggestions run on-device with Apple's Vision framework; no image is uploaded.
  • Microphone. Voice notes in messages are stored locally.
  • Location. If granted, used on-device to show nearby dishes and places; never transmitted.
  • Sign in with Apple. Used solely to personalise your local profile. Because we run no server, no credential, token or email is sent to us.
  • Notifications. Local reminders scheduled on your device; no push server; disable anytime in Settings.

You can decline camera, microphone, location and notification permissions and still use the app.

4. Purchases

Dishery offers optional paid upgrades ("Dishery Pro"). All purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple Account under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. We never see your payment details; the app checks your entitlement locally with Apple's StoreKit framework.

5. Sharing — only when you choose

Content leaves your device only when you explicitly share it via the system share sheet. What happens afterwards is governed by the destination you choose. Settings include a Data Export that produces a readable JSON file of everything you've made, generated on demand and not transmitted to us.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Deleting a dish, board, message or your profile removes it from the local database.
  • Signing out deletes your local profile.
  • Deleting the app deletes all Dishery data (run Data Export first if you want a copy).
  • Your device backups may include Dishery's local data under Apple's backup terms, which you control in Settings.

We cannot access, recover or delete your data on your behalf — we never have it.

7. Children

Dishery does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

8. Your rights (GDPR, CCPA and similar)

Dishery satisfies access, correction, deletion, portability and objection rights by design: all data is under your direct control on your device, portability is provided by Data Export, and there is no processing by us to object to.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds optional online features, this policy will be updated before any such feature ships, and any data practice will be reflected in the App Store privacy label.
