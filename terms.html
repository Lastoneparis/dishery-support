Reporting

Any post or account can be reported from inside the app, with a reason. Reports are recorded and reviewed by a person. We may remove content, restrict an account, or terminate it. If we remove something of yours and you ask why, we will tell you.

Blocking

Blocking is enforced on the server, not just hidden in the interface: once a block exists in either direction, neither of you can follow the other, comment, like, or start or continue a conversation. Existing follows are removed at the moment you block. You can see and undo your blocks in Settings → Blocked accounts.
